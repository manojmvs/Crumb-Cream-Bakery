Our story

A little bakery with a big heart.

Crumb & Cream began with a simple idea: make everyday baking feel special. We use quality ingredients, bake in small batches and put care into every detail — from the first whisk to the final sprinkle.

Whether it's a coffee-time cookie or a birthday cake for twenty, we believe good food should make people pause, smile and share.
